Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who is eligible to apply?

Anyone with a bachelor’s degree and at least 50% marks is eligible to apply. Candidates with less than 50% can also apply but must appear for the JSAT and complete a mandatory interview.

Is there an entrance test?

There is no separate entrance test. Admissions are based on a holistic evaluation of the applicant’s academic background, statement of purpose, and transcripts.
Shortlisted candidates may be invited for a brief personal interview, if required.

What is the programme fee structure?

The total tuition fee for the 1-year online M.Sc. in Global Risk Management programme is INR 2,75,000:

  • Registration Amount: INR 25,000 (upon acceptance of Offer Letter)
  • Instalment 1: INR 1,25,000 (before batch commencement)
  • Instalment 2: INR 1,25,000 (by 15th July 2027)

Flexible payment options are available for Indian nationals (Full Payment, Jodo Flex, and Jodo EMI 12 Months) and for foreign nationals (Full Payment and Two Instalment Payment Plan).

Are scholarships or financial aid available?

Yes, JGU offers a range of merit-cum-means scholarships, need-based financial aid, and special awards for diverse and international students. Scholarships cover tuition fees and are awarded based on academic performance, family income, and extracurricular achievements.

Is the programme aligned with the GARP Financial Risk Manager (FRM) certification?

Yes. The M.Sc. in Global Risk Management programme is aligned with the GARP Financial Risk Manager (FRM) curriculum. It covers all key domains (Market, Credit, Operational, and Regulatory Risks), providing a strong foundation for learners aspiring to pursue the FRM certification. This alignment enhances your professional credibility and prepares you for global risk management roles.

Are the classes conducted online or in person?

The programme is fully online, delivered in a quarterly format with live virtual classrooms, interactive sessions, and faculty mentorship.

What learning resources are available?

Learners gain access to:

  • Virtual labs and digital library resources
  • Recorded sessions and live lectures
  • Online support and mentoring throughout the programme
Can I work while studying?

Yes. The flexible online format is specifically designed for working professionals, allowing you to balance career and education without interruption.

What career opportunities does this programme prepare me for?

Graduates can pursue roles such as:

  • Risk Analyst
  • Enterprise Risk Manager
  • Credit & Market Risk Specialist
  • Compliance Officer
  • Risk Consultant
  • Quantitative Analyst

Career opportunities span across Banking, Insurance, Consulting, Fintech, and Regulatory sectors worldwide.
